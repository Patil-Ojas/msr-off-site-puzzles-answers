Riddle 01 — The Researcher

"A researcher who never read a single arxiv paper still knew everything worth knowing. His weekly paper count matched his weekly ROI. What was that number?"

Answer — 100
This is a classic misdirection riddle. The phrase "never read a single arxiv paper" is the red herring — the riddle is not asking about arxiv papers at all. It is asking about ROI, expressed as a percentage.

The clue is in "knew everything worth knowing" — this is a direct reference to the Efficient Researcher Hypothesis, a concept from the @vikhyatk tweet thread, which posits that a researcher operating at full informational efficiency achieves an ROI of 100% — i.e., every unit of time invested returns full value, with zero wasted on low-signal sources like arxiv preprints.

"His weekly paper count matched his weekly ROI" confirms the equivalence: his paper count (zero arxiv papers, but conceptually 100 units of useful output) maps to an ROI of 100. The number is 100.
